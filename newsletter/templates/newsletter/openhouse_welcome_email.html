Namaste. Jawafdehi (जवाफदेही) means accountability — an independent, open-source archive documenting corruption cases involving public entities in Nepal, evidence-first and free for everyone.

The Open House is a small, informal session over Zoom — usually ten to fifteen people. We talk about Nepal's accountability infrastructure, using AI and tech for good, and giving back to our home country. Come with questions, with ideas, or just to listen.

There's no fixed weekly time on purpose. Sessions are arranged around whoever is coming, so that people in Nepal, the Gulf, Asia, Europe, Africa, Australia and the Americas each get a slot that works for them. We'll email you when there's one for your region, with the time in your own, and again a few hours before it starts.

Jawafdehi Initiative

An independent, non-partisan, volunteer-led civic-tech platform documenting corruption cases involving public entities in Nepal. We document public accountability and raise awareness — we are not a court or legal authority.
